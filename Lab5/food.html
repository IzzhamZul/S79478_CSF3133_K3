About the Festival

Taste of Terengganu Food Festival is an exciting event that celebrates the traditional food and unique flavours of Terengganu. Visitors can enjoy various local dishes prepared by talented local food vendors.

The festival provides an opportunity for visitors to experience Terengganu's food culture, support local businesses and discover delicious traditional dishes such as Nasi Dagang, Keropok Lekor and Sata.

Free entry! Everyone is welcome to join the festival.
